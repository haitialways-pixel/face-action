Face-Action Treasure Hunt Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Face-Action Treasure Hunt ("the app", shown in French as "Face-Action Chasse au Trésor") is a game you play with touch or with facial expressions. This policy covers the Face-Action Treasure Hunt Android app and the web version at haitialways-pixel.github.io/face-action.

Camera

The app uses your device's front camera only while you play with face controls. The camera image is processed entirely on your device by an on-device face-tracking model (Google MediaPipe) to detect gestures such as blinks, head turns, mouth opening and smiles.

  • Video and images are never recorded, saved, or uploaded.
  • No face data, face geometry, or biometric identifiers are stored or sent anywhere.
  • The camera is turned off when you pause, when a rest break starts, when the game ends, or when you leave the app.

You can play with touch controls instead and never grant camera access. The app asks for the camera only after you choose “Play with Face” and read a short explanation.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking (the “Continue?” offer is currently a placeholder and shows no real ads), and it does not collect, share, or sell any personal data.

Data stored on your device

The app saves a few settings locally on your device: your high score, your calibration values (numbers describing your neutral expression and head-turn range, not images), sensitivity, volume, preview visibility, whether you've seen the How to Play screen, and game progress: your daily-challenge streak and daily scores, chapter missions, lifetime stats (such as guardians defeated), earned badges, unlocked helmet styles, and options such as Relaxed speed and shape outlines. Record cards you share are created on your device and only go where you choose to send them. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data (or your browser's site data) deletes it.

Network use

The Android app bundles everything it needs. The web version may download the app files and the face-tracking model from its host or a public CDN (jsDelivr / Google); those requests contain no personal or camera data.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
